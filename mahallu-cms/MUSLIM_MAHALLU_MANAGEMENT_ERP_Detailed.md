# MUSLIM MAHALLU MANAGEMENT ERP

## A Digital Framework for a Model Mahallu

**Functional Requirements (FR)**

> **Tagline:**\
> "Connecting Families. Strengthening Faith. Serving the Community."\
> **Know Every Family. Serve Every Need. Develop the Community.**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Vision

The **Muslim Mahallu Management ERP** is a comprehensive digital
platform designed to establish a systematic, transparent,
service-oriented, and community-focused model of Mahallu management.

The system digitally integrates:

-   Families
-   Mosque administration
-   Education
-   Welfare
-   Zakat
-   Finance
-   Employment
-   Youth
-   Women
-   Health
-   Counselling
-   Dispute resolution
-   Volunteering
-   Cemetery management
-   Community development

All of these areas are intended to operate within a **single integrated
platform**.

The objective is not merely to digitize existing Mahallu administration.
The ERP is intended to provide a practical digital framework for
developing an ideal and fully functional Mahallu.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. Core Objectives

The ERP shall help every Mahallu:

1.  Maintain a complete and updated demographic database.
2.  Know every family and its basic needs.
3.  Strengthen the relationship between families and the Mahallu.
4.  Improve mosque administration and services.
5.  Promote Islamic awareness and education.
6.  Ensure no deserving person is left without support.
7.  Systematize Zakat collection and distribution.
8.  Support education and employment.
9.  Promote economic self-reliance.
10. Provide family and marriage counselling.
11. Support elderly people, widows, orphans, and vulnerable families.
12. Develop youth and women's participation.
13. Organize volunteer and emergency services.
14. Maintain transparent financial records.
15. Promote harmony and social responsibility.
16. Measure the overall development of the Mahallu.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. Mahallu Classification

During registration, the system shall allow a Mahallu to be classified
as:

1.  **Fully Functional Mahallu**
2.  **Partially Functional Mahallu**
3.  **Urban Mosque**
4.  **Non-Jumu'ah Mosque / Musalla**

The ERP shall provide the complete set of Mahallu-management modules
primarily for **Fully Functional Mahallus**, while allowing appropriate
modules for other mosque categories.

# 35. Security & Privacy

Because the ERP will contain sensitive **personal, financial, health,
and welfare information**, the system must provide strong security and
privacy controls.

Required capabilities:

-   Role-based access
-   Data encryption
-   Secure authentication
-   Two-factor authentication
-   Audit logs
-   Backup
-   Disaster recovery
-   Access history
-   Data privacy controls
-   Restricted access to sensitive records

These controls are especially important for modules involving
counselling, health, welfare, finance, Zakat, family information, and
other sensitive records.

# 36. Future Multi-Mahallu Architecture

The system should be designed from the beginning to support expansion:

**One Mahallu → Multiple Mahallus → District → State-level Network**

The architecture can eventually provide aggregated and anonymized
insights into:

-   Education
-   Welfare
-   Zakat
-   Employment
-   Mosque development
-   Community services

This must happen **without exposing individual or sensitive family
information**.

# 38. Final Vision

The proposed ERP should ultimately create a **digital operating model
for an ideal Muslim Mahallu**.

It should enable the Mahallu to answer fundamental questions such as:

1.  Who are our families?
2.  What are their needs?
3.  Who needs education support?
4.  Who is unemployed?
5.  Who is eligible to receive Zakat?
6.  Who can contribute to the community?
7.  Which elderly people need support?
8.  Which children are at risk of dropping out?
9.  How are our finances being managed?
10. What has the Mahallu achieved this year?
11. What should we improve next year?

Thus, the ERP becomes more than an administrative software system.

It becomes a **digital infrastructure for building a knowledgeable,
morally responsible, economically stronger, socially caring, and
well-organized Mahallu**.
